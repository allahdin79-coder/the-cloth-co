Our Journey Towards Sustainable Fashion and Community Impact

At The Cloth Co., we are dedicated to circular fashion by bridging the gap between local grading and sorting capacity, and global supply networks. Through state-of-the-art recycling operation pipelines, we process commercial grade secondhand clothing, footwear, wiping rags, and household items.
