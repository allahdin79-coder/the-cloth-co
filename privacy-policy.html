Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

At The Cloth Co., operating from our headquarters and warehouse facilities in Sharjah, UAE, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the confidential data of our global B2B partners, buyers, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our wholesale export services.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate international wholesale transactions, shipping logistics, and inquiries, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal & Corporate Identification: Company name, contact person name, official business email addresses, telephone/mobile numbers, and physical business addresses.
  • Transaction & Shipping Data: Port of destination, container volume requirements, product preferences (such as used clothing grades, shoes, or wiping rags), and custom clearance documentation details.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and interaction metrics gathered through standard analytics cookies to optimize website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the gathered information exclusively for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Processing and responding to your wholesale quotation requests (RFQs).
  • Managing international freight forwarding, container shipping schedules, and export compliance documentation.
  • Communicating order updates, inspection reports (SGS/Intertek), and commercial invoices.
  • Improving our digital platform usability, service security, and customer support standards.

4. Data Security & Confidentiality

We implement stringent administrative, technical, and physical security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, data alteration, or disclosure of corporate and personal information. All transaction records and communication channels are maintained under strict commercial confidentiality standards.
