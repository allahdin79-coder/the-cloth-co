Classification Tiers

Strict Grading Classifications

Every single batch of secondhand apparel undergoes a rigorous hand-sorting process to guarantee clear, reliable quality grades.

Premium Grade A

Like-new garments with zero stains, tears, or fading. Features vibrant colors and trend-forward structural pattern for resale readiness.

Standard Grade B

Gently used clothing with minor, highly washable signs of use. A highly cost-effective option maintaining solid fabric integrity.

Industrial Grade C

Heavywear textiles suited for industrial wiping rags, ray-floc restoration, or direct ecological recycling initiatives.
